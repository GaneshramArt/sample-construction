How we build

Six stages. One team.

From the first survey to handover, you deal with one project director who knows every detail of your home.

i.

Consult

Site survey, brief, feasibility and an honest budget.

ii.

Design

Architecture, structure and interiors, drawn together.

iii.

Approve

Permits, RERA filings and a fixed build schedule.

iv.

Build

Our own site crews, with 140 quality checkpoints.

v.

Handover

A room-by-room walkthrough and complete documents.

vi.

Care

A ten-year structural warranty and a dedicated care line.

Engineering

Cantilevers, glass and concrete.

We engineer long-span cantilevers, curved curtain walls and exposed-concrete frames in-house, and our own crews build them.
